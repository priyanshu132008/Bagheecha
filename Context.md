# CONTEXT.md - Project Brief for Dev 1

## Brand Context: Hotel Bagheecha (Virar)
Hotel Bagheecha is a premier, medium-sized bar and restaurant in Virar known for high-volume dining, family gatherings, and night-out experiences. The goal of this website is to establish it as the single highest-rated lounge and dining experience in the city.

## The Three Seating Zones (Core Selling Point)
1. **The Terrace Lounge:** High-energy open-air rooftop setup, featuring signature cocktails, premium bottles, pot/hookah service, and lounge lighting.
2. **AC Fine Dining:** Cool, comfortable, family-friendly space built for quiet dinners and celebrations.
3. **Classic Dining (Non-AC):** Casual, fast-paced dining space.

## Core Page Sections to Build:
1. **Hero Canvas Section:** Immersive WebGL ambient/smoke background with cursor-following particle lights, bold serif headline, and instant CTAs.
2. **Atmosphere Showcase:** Interactive tab switcher/slider transitioning visually between Terrace, AC, and Non-AC vibes.
3. **Digital Menus:** Split view for **Food Menu** (Chef Specials, Triple Schezwan, Tandoori) and **Bar Menu** (Top Selling Bottles, Cocktails, Shisha/Pot).
4. **Table Reservation Modal:** High-converting, frictionless booking modal collecting Date, Time, Guests (Pax), and Zone Preference.
5. **Mobile Quick Action Bar:** Fixed bottom bar for instant calls, WhatsApp chat, and turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation.
